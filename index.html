Summer 2025 – Present

FPGA Accelerator for Ultrafast Ultrasound Beamforming

Graduate Research Assistant, Beckman Institute, UIUC

  • Vitis HLS
  • MATLAB
  • AMD Alveo V80
  • AVED
  • Versal NoC
  • PCIe/QDMA
  • Built a high-speed beamforming engine in Vitis HLS that turns raw signals from a 128-channel ultrasound probe into images, at over 12,000 acquisitions per second at 200 MHz.
  • Implemented a five-stage streaming pipeline of HLS kernels: data loading, 4× interpolation, transmit/receive delay compensation and multi-angle compounding, tuned for memory throughput and logic usage.
  • Developed a MATLAB-to-HLS verification flow that checks every pipeline stage against a reference model, using real ultrasound scanner data.
  • Integrating the engine into AMD’s Alveo V80 with AVED, routing data to high-speed memory through the Versal network-on-chip and to the host PC over PCIe/QDMA at over 10 GB/s.

Fall 2025

FPGA-Accelerated LeNet Inference on Zynq

  • C++
  • PYNQ-Z2
  • AXI
  • Vivado
  • Vitis HLS
  • Accelerated three convolution layers of a LeNet handwritten-digit classifier in C++/HLS at 100 MHz on the PYNQ-Z2, with AXI interfaces for ARM control and memory transfers.
  • Pipelined convolution loops, unrolled multiply-accumulate operations and reused data in on-chip buffers, for a 3.67× speedup over ARM software across 10,000 images.

Spring 2025

Out-of-Order RV32IM Processor

  • SystemVerilog
  • UVM
  • Synopsys VCS
  • Design Compiler
  • Co-designed a high-performance out-of-order RISC-V CPU in SystemVerilog with explicit register renaming (128 physical registers), a 32-entry reorder buffer, branch misprediction recovery and 4-way instruction and data caches, synthesized with Synopsys Design Compiler to meet timing and area targets.
  • Outperformed the course’s baseline processor in speed and power-performance on every benchmark, including CoreMark, raising IPC by over 10% with a GShare branch predictor (BTB, RAS), a next-line prefetcher and split load/store queues. Verified against Spike/RVFI and a coverage-driven UVM testbench with 100% RV32IM instruction coverage.
